Land of Ascii

the code on GitHub

A quiet walk through a land drawn in letters.

Hills, rivers, roofs, sheep and the people of the villages are made of characters, worked out as you look, by day and night and through the weather. Greet the villagers, do them small favours, sketch the views, and find your way by the signposts. Its sound and music are made in code as you go.

It is one HTML file, with no images and no engine, and it runs in your browser.

Play

WASD walk · mouse look · E greet · J journal · K sketch · M map · Esc everything else

Best with a keyboard and mouse, on a laptop or a desktop.
