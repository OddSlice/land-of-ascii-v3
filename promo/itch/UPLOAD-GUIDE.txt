LAND OF ASCII: the itch.io page, slot by slot
============================================

The sizes below come from community guides and itch.io moderators, not
from itch.io's own documentation. Before uploading each file, check the
size shown next to its slot on itch.io's upload screen. If one differs,
ask for the file at that size: the scripts make any of them again in
minutes.


1. THE GAME
   Upload: dist/land-of-ascii-0.6.5-itch.zip (index.html and play.html)
   Kind of project: HTML. Tick "This file will be played in the browser".
   Embed: 1280 x 720 or larger, with the fullscreen button on.
   Embed options: tick "SharedArrayBuffer support" if it's offered. The
   game then shares one copy of the world across its workers and runs
   at its best; without it, it still runs, on four workers.


2. THE PAGE'S IMAGES

   cover-630x500.png
     Slot: Cover image, on the game's edit page.
     The one people see in lists and search. It's made to read at half
     size (315 x 250), where itch.io often shows it.

   cover-animated-630x500.gif   (optional)
     Slot: the same Cover image slot, if you want it to move. itch.io
     plays an animated cover when someone hovers over it. Its frames
     match the still cover. 0.6 MB, a 5-second loop of the clouds and the
     water at dawn.
     Use either this or cover-630x500.png, not both.

   banner-960x300.png
     Slot: the banner, in the page's theme editor. It replaces the game's
     title at the top of the page, so it carries the name. If the title
     text still shows under the banner, look for the option there to hide it.

   wide-cover-1920x823.png
     Slot: the wide/featured cover (21:9), if your upload screen offers
     one. itch.io uses it when it features a game on its front page.

   background-tile-256.png
     Slot: the background image in the theme editor, set to repeat. It is the
     game's dark field with faint letters, and it repeats without a seam.

   screenshots/1-meeting-on-the-road.png ... 6-a-sketch.png
     Slot: Screenshots, on the game's edit page, in this order:
       1  Rose Cobb meets you on the road with her favour
       2  Elmswick at dusk, as the journal notes the village
       3  the journal's Places page
       4  the map after a day's walk
       5  a signpost read on the road
       6  a sketch, open in the journal
     They show the game exactly as a player sees it, at 1920 x 1080, with
     two Display settings a player can choose: text "two steps larger"
     and statistics off.


3. THE TRAILER
   itch.io's trailer field only takes a YouTube or Vimeo link; it can't
   take a video file. Upload promo/video/land-of-ascii-trailer.mp4 to
   YouTube (Unlisted is fine) or Vimeo, then paste the link into the
   trailer field on the game's edit page.


4. THEME COLOURS (the theme editor), taken from the game itself

   Background             #0d0e12   the game's dark (behind the tile)
   Background 2 / column  #131419   the menu's side panel
   Text                   #dcd7cb   the game's ink
   Headings               #f0e6cc   the brighter ink of names and titles
   Links                  #ffb060   the game's orange (the Play button)
   Buttons                #ffb060   with button text #1d1409
   Borders                #383842   the panels' edges

   The stone and timber of the frame, if a field asks for an accent:
   stone #b8ae9c (lit) and #56524f (shade); timber #795939 and #35271b.

   Font: if itch.io's font list has a monospace font, pick it. The game
   itself uses Cascadia Mono.


5. WORDS FOR THE PAGE
   One line:  A quiet walk through a land drawn in letters.
   Then:      Meet the villagers, do them favours and sketch the views.
   Recent updates (two sentences): promo/CHANGELOG.txt
