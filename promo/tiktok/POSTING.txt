LAND OF ASCII on TikTok: three videos, ready to post
==================================================

All three are 1080 x 1920 (9:16), 60 frames a second, with the game's own
music and sounds, levelled to -14 LUFS, a common loudness target for
TikTok and Reels. They work as they are on Instagram Reels and YouTube
Shorts too.

Every caption and the end card keep to the middle and upper part of the
picture, clear of TikTok's buttons on the right and its caption area at
the bottom. The end card says PLAY FREE IN YOUR BROWSER, ODDSLICE.ITCH.IO
and "link in bio".


BEFORE YOU POST
  - Put https://oddslice.itch.io in your TikTok bio. Links in captions
    aren't clickable on TikTok, which is why the videos say "link in bio".
  - The sound is the game's own, so TikTok will list it as "original
    sound". If your app offers to rename it, call it "Land of Ascii", so
    anyone who uses the sound is led back to the game.
  - The text is burned into the videos. If you add TikTok's own captions
    as well, keep them short so they don't cover the end card.


1. land-of-ascii-tiktok-letters.mp4   (14 s)   the hook
   Opens on the land in huge letters, which sharpen four times into the
   real game. Then a village at midday and the fort at night.
   On screen: "This whole world is drawn in letters." / "Everyone here
   keeps a day of their own." / "No enemies. Just a quiet walk."
   Cover frame: the first second (the big letters).
   Suggested caption:
     Every square on screen is a letter. Land of Ascii is a quiet walk
     through a land drawn in text. Free in your browser, link in bio.

2. land-of-ascii-tiktok-day.mp4   (15 s)   the timelapse
   Elmswick from 05:00 to about 21:45, a clock on screen. Sunrise, the
   midday light, the sunset and the lamps coming on.
   On screen: "A whole day in a village made of letters."
   Cover frame: around 8.5 s (the sunset, about 18:15).
   Suggested caption:
     A day in Elmswick, sped up. The sky, the clouds and the lamps are
     all letters. Free to play in your browser, link in bio.

3. land-of-ascii-tiktok-favour.mp4   (22 s)   the story
   Rose Cobb meets you on the road and asks you to take a mended cloak to
   the fort. You head there at sunset. At the gate, Juliana Rush thanks
   you and gives you a lantern for the road.
   On screen: "POV: a stranger stops you on the road." / "Carry it to the
   fort before dark." / "A small kindness, and a lantern for the road."
   Cover frame: around 5 s (Rose's request).
   Suggested caption:
     Rose asked me to take a mended cloak up to the fort before dark.
     Land of Ascii, a quiet game drawn in letters. Link in bio.

To make them again after an update (from the repository's folder):
  npm run build
  node tools/promo/tiktok.mjs                  all three (about 3 minutes each)
  node tools/promo/tiktok.mjs --only day       one of them
  node tools/promo/check-trailer.mjs promo/tiktok/land-of-ascii-tiktok-day.mp4 --seconds 15 --size 1080x1920
