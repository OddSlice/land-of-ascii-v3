LAND OF ASCII: five peaceful walks for TikTok
============================================

Five vertical videos, 1080 x 1920 (9:16), 30 seconds each, 60 frames a
second. No captions, no end card: just the walk, inside the game's stone
frame, fading in from the dark and out again. Each has the game's own
sound (its music, the wind, the water, birds, footsteps), levelled to
-14 LUFS. They were recorded fresh from the game as it is today.

1. land-of-ascii-walk-lake-to-village.mp4
   Along the shore of Dunholme's lake in the afternoon, the village
   mirrored in the water, then up the road into the village.

2. land-of-ascii-walk-fort-weather.mp4
   The fort on its hill above the winding road, from dawn to night in 30
   seconds: clear skies, cloud, rain, mist at dusk, then a clear night
   with the gate's torches lit. (Sound: the wind, the rain and the music.)

3. land-of-ascii-walk-flock.mp4
   A slow walk in among the grazing flock on a morning with a little mist,
   the shepherd beyond.

4. land-of-ascii-walk-bridge.mp4
   Down the fort road and over the stone bridge across the Fennwater, late
   in the afternoon, on toward Dunholme with the sun low ahead.

5. land-of-ascii-walk-night-village.mp4
   The road into Elmswick under the stars, lantern in hand, the windows and
   the lamps lit.


BEFORE YOU POST
  - Make sure https://oddslice.itch.io is in your TikTok bio: these videos
    carry no words at all, so the caption has to say where to play.
  - A caption idea for any of them:
      A quiet walk in Land of Ascii, a world drawn entirely in letters.
      Free in your browser, link in bio.
  - Hashtags to choose from:
      #cozygames #indiegame #asciiart #relaxing #ambience #gamedev
      #indiedev #browsergame #pixelart #itchio


To make them again after an update (from the repository's folder):
  npm run build
  node tools/promo/tiktok-walks.mjs                       all five (about 5 minutes each)
  node tools/promo/tiktok-walks.mjs --only fort-weather   one of them
