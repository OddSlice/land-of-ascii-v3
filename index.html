Land of Ascii

A quiet walk through a land drawn in letters.

Walk where you like

The hills, the rivers, the sheep and the villagers are all drawn in characters. The game works each one out as you look, day or night, whatever the weather.

Greet people as you pass, and some will ask you a small favour. Sketch the views you like. The signposts point the way between villages. Even the sound and music are made in code while you play.

It's a single HTML file with no images and no engine. Play it with a keyboard and mouse.

the keys

  • walk WASD
  • look round mouse
  • greet E
  • journal J
  • sketch K
  • map M
  • everything else Esc
